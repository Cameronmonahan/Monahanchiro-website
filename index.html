Good to know

Questions people usually ask us

No pressure, no jargon — just honest answers before you come in.

What actually happens during an adjustment?

We use gentle, hands-on techniques to help restore normal movement to joints in your spine and elsewhere. Most patients feel looser and more mobile right after — some notice it takes a visit or two to feel the full effect.

Do you treat kids and older adults?

Yes — we adjust our approach based on age and health, and we've cared for patients from toddlers to grandparents. It's one of the reasons whole families keep coming back.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on what's going on. Some people come in for a specific issue and feel better in a few visits; others prefer ongoing care to stay ahead of stiffness. We'll talk through what makes sense for you — no set package you're pushed into.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans. Give us a call and we're happy to check your coverage before your first visit.

Do I need a referral to see you?

No referral needed. You can call or stop by directly, and we're happy to answer questions before you book anything.
